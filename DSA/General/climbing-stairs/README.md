## 📝 Problem Statement

Given an integer n, there is a staircase with n steps, starting from the 0^th step.

Determine the number of **unique ways** to reach the n^th step, given that each move can be either 1 or 2 steps at a time.

### Example 1:

**Input:** n = 2

**Output:** 2

**Explanation:**

There are 2 unique ways to climb to the 2nd step:

1) 1 step + 1 step

2) 2 steps

### Example 2:

**Input:** n = 3

**Output:** 3

**Explanation:**

There are 3 unique ways to climb to the 3rd step:

1) 1 step + 1 step + 1 step

2) 2 steps + 1 step

3) 1 step + 2 steps
